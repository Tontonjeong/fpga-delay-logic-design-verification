# Provenance

The original source package was present in the supplied workspace as a ZIP archive. The public portfolio copy is based on the authored files in that package:

- `delay_logic.sv`
- `tb_delay_logic.sv`
- ModelSim DO scripts
- Quartus QPF/QSF/Tcl files
- 100 MHz SDC
- expected-waveform tables and figures

The original ZIP and DOCX report are not committed. Public-copy changes are limited to directory-relative paths, professional wording, a common Agilex 5 target device, and reproducibility wrappers. The RTL and testbench were not reconstructed from prose.

The included waveform figures are labeled **Expected Waveform** and are not presented as ModelSim captures. No preserved ModelSim transcript or Quartus report was supplied, and neither commercial tool was available in the portfolio assembly environment.
